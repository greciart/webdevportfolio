This site is the portfolio of **Grecia V.**, a freelance web designer and frontend developer. I am responsible for the personal data described here. The short version: I collect as little as I can, I never sell it, and analytics only run if you agree to them.

## What I collect and why

### When you contact me

The contact form asks for your **name**, your **email** and a **message**. The service and budget fields are optional. The form also records how long it was open, which helps filter out spam bots.

I use this only to reply to you and, if we work together, to prepare a quote and manage the project. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a possible contract. The form is processed by **Netlify Forms** and reaches my inbox by email. I keep messages for as long as the conversation or project needs them, and I delete them sooner if you ask. The same applies if you email me directly.

### Analytics, only with your consent

If you click **Accept** on the cookie notice, the site loads **Microsoft Clarity**. It shows me which pages people visit, where they click and how far they scroll, so I can fix what is confusing. Clarity collects device and browser details, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and anonymous session recordings. Text you type into fields is masked and never recorded.

The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer. If you reject, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Clarity never loads. Microsoft's handling of this data is described in the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

### Spam protection

When it is switched on, the contact form uses **Google reCAPTCHA** to tell people from bots. It only loads once you start filling in the form, and Google receives technical information about your browser and device. The [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) apply.

### Hosting

The site is hosted on **Netlify**. Like any web server, it processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. This is a legitimate interest in running a secure website.

### Videos

Embedded videos load nothing from YouTube or Vimeo until you press play. YouTube videos use the privacy-enhanced `youtube-nocookie.com` domain, and Vimeo is asked not to track you.

### Preferences saved on your device

Your theme, accessibility settings, blog reactions and cookie choice are stored in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and I cannot see them. Details are in the [Cookie Policy](/cookies/).

## What I do not do

- I do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising.
- I do not add you to a mailing list because you wrote to me.
- I do not use advertising trackers or social media pixels.

## Where your data goes

Netlify, Microsoft and Google may process data in the United States. They rely on recognized safeguards for international transfers, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Links to other sites, such as Contra, Ko-fi, Calendly, Fiverr or LinkedIn, take you to services with their own privacy policies.

## Your rights

You can ask me to:

- see the personal data I hold about you;
- correct it if it is wrong;
- delete it;
- restrict or object to how I use it;
- send it to you in a portable format;
- withdraw your consent to analytics at any time.

You can also file a complaint with the data protection authority where you live. I would appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.

## Children

This site is not aimed at children under 16, and I do not knowingly collect their data.

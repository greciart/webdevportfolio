Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. This site sets **no cookies of its own**. The only cookies that can appear come from Microsoft Clarity, and only after you accept them.

## Analytics cookies (need your consent)

These are set by **Microsoft Clarity** only if you click **Accept** on the cookie notice.

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Lasts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_clck` | Microsoft Clarity | Recognizes your browser between visits with a random ID | 1 year |
| `_clsk` | Microsoft Clarity | Groups the pages you view into one session | 1 day |
| `MUID` | Microsoft | Identifies your browser across Microsoft services | 1 year |

## Necessary cookies

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Lasts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_GRECAPTCHA` | Google reCAPTCHA | Tells people from spam bots on the contact form. Only set when reCAPTCHA is switched on and you start filling in the form. | 6 months |

## Saved on your device, never sent anywhere

These are not cookies. They live in your browser's local storage, they never travel with a request, and I cannot see them.

| Key | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `theme` | Remembers light or dark mode |
| `a11y-prefs` | Remembers your accessibility settings |
| `reactions:…` | Remembers which reactions you left on a blog post |
| `cookie-consent` | Remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics, for 12 months |

## Changing your choice

You can change your mind at any time:

- <button type="button" data-consent-open class="cursor-pointer font-semibold text-mint-500 underline transition-all hover:text-riptide-400 dark:text-riptide-300">Open cookie settings</button>, also available in the footer of every page.
- Delete this site's cookies and storage from your browser settings. The notice will appear again on your next visit.

If you reject analytics after accepting them, the page reloads without Clarity and its cookies on this domain are deleted. If your browser sends a **Global Privacy Control** signal, it is treated as a rejection and Clarity never loads.

More on how your data is handled is in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
